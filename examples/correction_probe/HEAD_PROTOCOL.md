# One head-path development diagnostic

**Final goal.** A patch-net equilibrium brain that is more scalable, more capable and more efficient than a transformer. Every Cadence result is measured against that goal at matched information, matched task and a declared resource model. The Amen jungle composer is the first test platform.

**Principles.** The main hypothesis stays: a brain that settles in global equilibria. The building block stays as simple as possible, like in nature, and every part of the brain answers with a settled state of that same patch rule; a feed-forward readout or a copied input is a baseline, never a result. Natural evolution is preferred to design: any parameter that looks designed is a gene, picked by selection against the hand-set value, which stays as the control. Within a life only the patch rule learns; across lives the genome evolves.

This single development change follows the failed/capped seed-3109 preflight
and its separately verified saved-state sensitivity diagnostic. Direct raw F
drive averaged about .19–.22 in absolute value, versus about .001 from G.
Those measurements motivate a candidate wiring gene; they do not prove a
cause, certify a useful observer effect, or make this viewed seed confirmation.
The original sources, results and 60-second wide censor remain unchanged.

Run exactly five arms, all seed 3109: head ordinary, head observer, head wide,
original raw-bypass ordinary, original raw-bypass observer. The first three
remove only direct raw contacts into F; F now reads nonlinear G alone. G still
receives all 13 raw actor coordinates plus H/P/C, so every layout has identical
factual information. Original raw ordinary remains a capable comparison, even
if removing the bypass harms ordinary acquisition. Counts are 12/140, 12/142,
20/304, 12/153 and 12/155 states/parameters. Paired numerical seeds do not
promise identical shared coefficients across different compiled graphs.

The [original protocol](PROTOCOL.md) supplies unchanged physics, partial
measurement boundary, data, shuffled schedules, 128×16 teaching presentations,
checks at 0/32/64/128, four 64-step branches and two actual-witness fits per
fitting branch. The withheld F measures the current post-command state; this
is completion with delayed reveal, not future action control. No new hidden
label, fabricated error, optimizer, attention trigger or learning dose is added.
All seven public mathematical modules remain pinned. Exact raw-control founder
and acquired snapshot parity with the original run is required.

All five arms must complete and acquire routine and corrected MAE ≤ .04 and
halve newborn error. Head observer's corrected/no-fit error over the 48
post-disturbance rows must be at least 10% and .002 below each of head ordinary,
head wide and raw ordinary, and at least 10% below its own routine error.
Raw observer is a descriptive control. Original old-skill retention gates
(.005 maximum clean MAE increase, 10% shifted reduction) are reported for every
arm without modification. Full acquisition/query/fitting work and refusals
are retained; greater quality at greater work is not an efficiency result.

Every new arm has a prospective 120-second main-operation cap and 125-second
external process cap; serial execution takes at most about 625 seconds plus
bounded setup/bookkeeping. This does not retroactively complete the original
wide case. Each complete arm has 132 admissions and 1,296 pure queries.
All five outcomes, unknown interrupted work, and 70-MB between-arm/final
storage eligibility checks are retained. There is no automatic seed, width,
dose or threshold expansion if this one change is insufficient. Numerical
learning replay is a separate verification task, not implied by collection.
