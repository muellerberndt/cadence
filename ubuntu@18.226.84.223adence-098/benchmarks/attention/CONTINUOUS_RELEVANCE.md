# Shared continuous-payload relevance component

This component assay asks whether one live coupled read learns to select the fact requested by the present question. The shared scalar payload changes on every round and in a separate held sample. All four capacity controls pass, but both learned founders fail acquisition and causal relevance. The original failed tasks, receipts and acceptance criteria remain unchanged.

The nine input coordinates are name A/B at 0/1, a shared scalar quantity at 2, fact marker at 3, neutral at 4, ask A/B at 5/6, and the exposed control's two quantities at 7/8. A fact carries its name and actual quantity; the later question carries only its ask marker. The exposed control receives both actual unselected quantities. The final binary answer is the class of the asked quantity, with threshold .5. The teacher supplies only that final answer on wrong rows. It supplies no address, selector or head-role label.

Each draw contains the four A/B class pairs in both presentation orders: eight actual histories and sixteen paired questions. Low quantities lie in [.1,.4] and high quantities in [.6,.9]. Quantities are independent continuous draws for every history and round; row positions are independently shuffled. Both questions reuse the same actual quantities and prefix. Scorer metadata never enters observations. Train, held and donor streams have independent deterministic namespaces, and receipts retain the full actual paired task records and their joint hashes.

Training repeats each actual prefix into two streams before the paired questions. Evaluation witnesses each eight-history prefix once, then forks that host for both questions and conditions. The query boundary clears working trace and warm activity while retaining the actual witnessed bank. This is an explicit reset-round component boundary; “continuous” describes the payload, not a continuing creature. There is no learned meaning code, episodic lifecycle or integrated-life claim.

The candidate is a direct nine-input, two-action, one-output-slot, one-head `MultiHeadBrain`: 32 association neurons, ordinary query founder initialization once, total memory coupling 3, temperature .25, empty anchor, dt1, native qualified 2048-step settlement with damping3/tolerance.003, beta.1, eta.003 and RMS/momentum settings inherited unchanged from the frozen instrument. The query and read remain live during the same settle. The query/address is not taught separately. The raw9 exposed, supplied-address and learned-head constructors have 75, 93 and 93 neurons respectively.

The supplied-address control chooses the latest actual held fact of the requested name from its name and fact-marker coordinates. It fixes the complete actual stored value as its read target, with the same alpha3 restoring force; prefixes and absent facts select null. It neither invents a target payload nor calls the old raw11 oracle constructor. This explicit oracle measures content-use capacity, not learned relevance.

A new four-cell capacity stage runs exposed and supplied-address controls for development founders 0 and 1. Only its verified positive receipt admits the two learned H1 cells. Each cell uses 512 rounds of sixteen paired questions. Both learned founders are attempted even if the first fails. Actual neutral events alternate amplitude1/.5 by global round, identically for every arm, including conditional retention. There is one declared model point, no parameter search, no automatic retries or confirmation seeds, and no world or AWS stage.

The predeclared held sample contains 32 fresh eight-history blocks: 512 answers, 256 per question and 128 unequal-fact histories per question. Every newborn intact block must qualify; newborn accuracy is unrestricted. Acquisition requires complete qualified training and held evaluation, preserved parameters and optimizer state during evaluation, and at least .95 accuracy overall, per question, per presentation order and on unequal facts. Unavailable or refused queries remain unanswered errors.

The learned H1 gates additionally require:

- Actual requested-record mass at least .5 and margin at least .1 over every other held record and null, on at least .8 of unequal histories for each question: at least 103/128 per question.
- At least .8 donor-follow accuracy for each question when the relevant actual fact changes class. Changing the irrelevant actual fact across its class boundary must change at most .05 of answers for each question.
- Erasing the relevant record must reduce original accuracy by at least .2 for each question. Halving the actual neutral K/V, erasing only its held flag, and jointly reordering K/V/held/ages must each change at most .05 of answers per question.
- Freezing or shuffling the acquired interface must each reduce overall accuracy by at least .2; the corresponding per-question losses are also retained. Every required causal panel must complete and qualify. A refused lesion cannot manufacture a useful loss.

Donors are independently witnessed actual alternate histories. Each intervention transplants the chosen named fact's actual K and V together, preserving host query drive, held flags, ages and other records. `all_erased` is an unsupported/null diagnostic, excluded from admission; binary motors have no abstention label.

A founder passing all primary gates receives 256 same-distribution continuation rounds, global indices512..767. Its post-retention held reading uses independent blocks32..63 and must meet all acquisition floors with overall loss at most .05. This is same-distribution continuation, not retention under competing learning.

The source-bound protocol first verifies the completed neutral-variation H2 receipt, its complete dependency closure and cumulative cost5512.356352962387 host seconds. That earlier result passed content and nuisance gates but failed distinct useful-head specialization. The new component does not change that result or establish a head-count benefit. Capacity has a 600-second stage envelope and 240-second training-call budget; learned H1 has 1800 seconds and 600 seconds per training call. Budgets are checked between rounds and panels, so a separate watchdog is required for a hard host cap.

The controller retains actual prefix/query/teacher/refusal work and cortical and memory transport counters. Atomic progress files record phase boundaries and every32 completed training rounds; they do not change learning or enable resume. Final, conditional-retention and interrupted snapshots contain the three parameters, all four optimizer arrays, counters, source hashes and actual source revision. They are research arrays for reset rounds, not supported continuing checkpoints. Interrupted work and elapsed cost remain recorded.

The scalar payload prevents separate A-value/B-value coordinates from directly delivering two independent scalars in one fixed read. Fresh values remove the earlier four exact binary codepoints. This is still an empirical generalization and causal-use test, not a proof that majority selection is necessary. Soft name masses can encode a quantity difference; together with their scalar mixture they can retain both quantities. Pure tests preserve that counterexample and a fixed equal-mixture ambiguity. A broader useful-soft-view criterion would need its own prospective protocol and measurements.

Constructor and instrument tests forbid graph equilibration. They cover actual Recall storage, paired-bank custody, native wrong-row teaching inputs, donor and sham changes, exact work census, live-query selector recomputation, source/revision/prior gates, full research snapshots, progress and interruption accounting. Pure task tests forbid model imports. Passing these software tests will not count as a behavioral result.

## Measured result

The frozen source is `37436c4feeef002a792aa49e628437f997928a4b`.
All four [capacity controls](results/continuous-relevance-capacity-summary.json)
complete 512 training rounds and answer all 512 fresh questions correctly,
including each question, presentation order and unequal-fact subset.
Both learned H1 founders also complete 512 rounds without an action or teaching
refusal, but their [held readings](results/continuous-relevance-h1-summary.json)
fail the required 95% floors:

| Learned founder | Overall | Ask A | Ask B | Unequal facts |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 69.5% | 79.3% | 59.8% | 54.3% |
| 1 | 66.6% | 64.8% | 68.4% | 43.4% |

Changing the irrelevant actual fact changes 39.1%/21.9% of A/B answers in
founder 0 and 31.6%/61.3% in founder 1. Both have zero qualifying selective
histories out of 128 unequal histories for each question. Neither passes the
acquisition, causal, interface or selection gates. Conditional retention stays
unrun. Concentration is a sufficient operational criterion rather than a
necessity theorem, but dropping it would not repair these functional failures.

424.0804257921409, bringing the complete recorded campaign ledger to
